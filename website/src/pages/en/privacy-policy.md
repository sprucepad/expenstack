# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 31, 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the App ("we," "our," or "the App") handles your information when you use our personal expense tracking application.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. The App is designed to function entirely on your device and does not collect, transmit, or share your personal information with us or any third parties.

## Information Stored by the App

The App allows you to record and manage personal financial information, including:

- Expense records
- Income records
- Categories and tags
- Notes you choose to enter
- Other financial data you voluntarily add

This information is stored locally on your device and is used solely to provide the App's functionality.

## No Data Collection

We do not collect, receive, store, or process any personal information on our servers because the App does not communicate your data to us.

Specifically, we do not:

- Collect account information
- Require user registration
- Collect contact information
- Collect device identifiers
- Track user activity
- Use analytics services
- Use advertising SDKs
- Collect location data
- Monitor app usage

## Local Storage

All data entered into the App remains stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data and cannot view, retrieve, modify, or delete it.

Because the data remains on your device, you are responsible for:

- Maintaining backups if desired
- Securing your device
- Managing any device-level security features such as passwords, biometrics, or encryption

## Data Sharing

The App does not share your information with us or with third parties.

Any data export, backup, or sharing functionality that may be provided by your device's operating system or services is controlled by you and subject to the privacy policies of those services.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, tracking services, or data collection services.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because no information is transmitted to us.

## Data Security

Since all information remains on your device, the security of your data depends primarily on the security measures of your device and operating system.

We recommend keeping your device updated and using available security features such as screen locks, passwords, or biometric authentication.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the contact information provided in the App or on the App's distribution page.
